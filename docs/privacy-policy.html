Arrowgami Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Arrowgami is a puzzle game made by Asylum MKE LLC ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We don't ask for your name, email or any account. Your level progress, stars, hints and settings are saved only on your device. We don't receive them.

Ads

Arrowgami shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location (based on your IP address), information about your device, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in Google's advertising policy and privacy policy.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for personalized ads. If you say no, you still get ads, but they aren't personalized.
  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice anytime with Privacy choices on the game's home screen.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads and hint packs are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game only which items you own.

Children

Arrowgami isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
